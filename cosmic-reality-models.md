<h2>CORE ISSUES IN QUANTUM MECHANICS &amp; PHYSICS</h2><div><br /></div>
<div><strong>The Core Dilemma</strong></div><div><strong><br /></strong></div>
<div></div>
<p>The relationship and conflict between the properties of a <strong>Particle/Atom</strong> (localized, static, discrete) and a <strong>Wave</strong> (spread out, uncertain, probability-based).</p><p><br /></p>
<div><strong>The Measurement Problem</strong></div><div><strong><br /></strong></div>
<div></div>
<p>How a spread-out quantum wavefunction collapses into a definite particle/atomic point during physical interaction (<strong>Decoherence</strong>). This is driven by physical interactions, <strong>NOT</strong> by human eyesight or consciousness.</p><p><br /></p>
<h2>THE DEFINITION OF "OBSERVER" &amp; THE UNCERTAINTY PRINCIPLE</h2><div><br /></div>
<div><strong>Observer</strong></div><div><strong><br /></strong></div>
<div></div>
<p>Any detector or macroscopic physical object that interacts with a particle. It does not refer to the human mind or consciousness.</p><p><br /></p>
<div><strong>Heisenberg’s Uncertainty Principle</strong></div><div><strong><br /></strong></div>
<div></div>
<p>A fundamental physical limit of the universe. It is not merely a mechanical disturbance during measurement (i.e., the instrument disturbing the particle), but an inherent, natural property of waves. Obtaining full, simultaneous knowledge of both position and momentum is fundamentally impossible.</p><p><br /></p>
<h2>HISTORICAL EVOLUTION &amp; PHYSICS TERMINOLOGY</h2><div><br /></div>
<div><strong>Classical Physics (Pre-1900)</strong></div><div><strong><br /></strong></div>
<div></div>
<p>Newton &amp; Maxwell — The universe is deterministic, orderly, and predictably certain.</p><p><br /></p>
<div><strong>Modern Physics (~126 Years, Founded in 1900)</strong></div><div><strong><br /></strong></div>
<div></div>
<p>Planck, Einstein, Bohr, Heisenberg, Schrödinger — Quantum Mechanics and Relativity.</p><p><br /></p>
<div><strong>The Formative Era of Quantum Mechanics (1900–1935)</strong></div><div><strong><br /></strong></div>
<div></div>
<p>Spans from Planck’s energy quanta (1900), De Broglie’s Hypothesis (1924: Particles behave as Waves), the Davisson-Germer Electron Diffraction Experiment (1927), to Schrödinger’s Cat Thought Experiment (1935).</p><p><br /></p>
<div><strong>Bell’s Theorem (1964) &amp; the 2022 Nobel Prize</strong></div><div><strong><br /></strong></div>
<div></div>
<p>Established experimental proof that the universe cannot be <strong>Local</strong> and <strong>Realistic</strong> at the same time. This proves that old-school classical determinism (<strong>Local Realism</strong>) is physically impossible.</p><p><br /></p>
<h2>QUALIA VS. OBJECTIVE REALITY</h2><div><br /></div>
<div><strong>Qualia / Subjective Perception</strong></div><div><strong><br /></strong></div>
<div></div>
<p>Individual sensory experiences (e.g., a sighted person visualizing an atom vs. a blind person processing space through touch and sound).</p><p><br /></p>
<div><strong>Objective Reality</strong></div><div><strong><br /></strong></div>
<div></div>
<p>The physical truth of the universe that exists and reacts according to the laws of physics, completely independent of how the human mind visualizes it.</p><p><br /></p>
<h2>SYNTHESIS OF ANCIENT &amp; MODERN PHILOSOPHY</h2><div><br /></div>
<div><strong>Heraclitus (Flux/Panta Rhei)</strong></div><div><strong><br /></strong></div>
<div></div>
<p>Nature is an ever-flowing river (proto-wave) governed by an orderly law (The Wave Equation).</p><p><br /></p>
<div><strong>Nagarjuna</strong></div><div><strong><br /></strong></div>
<div></div>
<p>The quantum realm cannot be confined to the binary logic of "Exists", "Does Not Exist", "Both", or "Neither". Reality is <strong>Sunyata</strong> (empty of independent, inherent existence).</p><p><br /></p>
<div><strong>Theravada Abhidhamma</strong></div><div><strong><br /></strong></div>
<div></div>
<p>Reality "flashes" or blinks at a frequency of trillions of moments per second, unifying <strong>Sabhava</strong> (atomic characteristics) with <strong>Anicca/Paticcasamuppada</strong> (the conditional flow of waves).</p><p><br /></p>
<h2>5 MODELS OF THE COSMIC REALITY MAP (A Summary of the History of Thought)</h2><div><br /></div>
<div><strong>1. The Particle Block / "Static Marble" Model (Classical Atomism / Newton)&nbsp;</strong></div><div><strong><br /></strong></div>
<div></div>
<p>Reality is built from hard, solid, and deterministic physical building blocks.</p><p><br /></p>
<div><strong>2. The Wave / "Flow &amp; Vibration" Model (Huygens, Maxwell, Heraclitus)</strong></div><div><strong><br /></strong></div>
<div></div>
<p>There are no static objects; reality consists of motion patterns, ripples, and processes of change.</p><p><br /></p>
<div><strong>3. The Dualism / "Both Simultaneously" Model (Bohr, De Broglie, Daoism)</strong></div><div><strong><br /></strong></div>
<div></div>
<p>Particles and waves are two different facets of the same underlying quantum reality (<strong>Complementarity</strong>).</p><p><br /></p>
<div><strong>4. The Interdependent Model (Nagarjuna, Abhidhamma, Carlo Rovelli)</strong></div><div><strong><br /></strong></div>
<div></div>
<p>Discrete entities do not exist; there are only relationships, events, and conditional processes (<strong>Relational Quantum Mechanics</strong>).</p><p><br /></p>
<div><strong>5. The Digital / "Matrix Simulation" Model (Wheeler's "It from Bit" / Information Physics)</strong></div><div><strong><br /></strong></div>
<div></div>
<p>Matter and energy are fundamentally based on digital code or data information processed on the screen of reality.</p>
