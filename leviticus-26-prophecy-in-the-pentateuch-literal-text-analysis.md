The Mechanics of Voice</b></h2><br /><b>Deuteronomy 30:</b> This is mediated, indirect speech. Moses is the narrator using the first-person ("I am commanding you today") and refers to YHWH in the third person ("the LORD your God will restore you").<br /><br /><b>Leviticus 26:</b> This is legit, verbatim direct speech from YHWH via Moses ("I will scatter you... I Myself will lay waste").<br /><br /><b>The Verdict:</b> There is no narrative passage in the entire Pentateuch where YHWH speaks directly to the mass assembly of Israel in first-person speech after the Sinai event.<br /><br /><br /><br /><br /><h2 style="text-align: left;"><b>2. The Legal Framework</b></h2><br /><b>Deuteronomy:</b> Uses a "legal fiction" via the second-person masculine singular pronoun ("you" / attah). This grammatically collapses generations to legally bind unborn descendants to the treaty.<br /><br /><b>Leviticus 26:</b> Contains no legal fiction. It uses standard second-person masculine plural grammar ("you all") and explicitly separates the generations (separating "you" from "your ancestors").<br /><br /><br /><b><br /></b><div><h2 style="text-align: left;"><b>3. Leviticus 26:40-46 Literal Text Verdict </b></h2><br /><b>Verses 40-41:</b> Prophesies that Israel will confess their sins and their uncircumcised hearts will be humbled while trapped in enemy lands.<br /><br /><b>Verse 42:</b> States exactly "and the land I will remember." It does not mention a physical return, gathering, or relocation.<br /><br /><b>Verse 44-45:</b> Confirms 100% that no physical homecoming is promised in this chapter. YHWH guarantees contract maintenance and survival ("I will not reject them so as to destroy them utterly"), but leaves them physically in the land of their enemies.<br /><br /><b>Verse 46:</b> Functions as a formal legal colophon closing the document.<br /><br /><br /><br /><br /><b>Leviticus 26</b> is a unique, literal direct speech from YHWH that completely avoids the legal fictions of Deuteronomy, and it guarantees that Israel will survive and the covenant will stay active, but it promises 100% no physical return from the land of their enemies.</div>
